PawPantry ships pet food, litter and accessories from our logistics centre in Lyon, France, to the countries listed on this page. Prices below include VAT and are charged per order, not per item.

Dispatch times and order cut-off

Orders placed and paid before 14:00 CET, Monday to Friday, leave our warehouse the same business day. Orders placed after 14:00, on weekends or on French public holidays are dispatched on the next business day.

Orders paid by bank transfer are dispatched once the payment has reached our account (see Payment Methods). Pre-order items ship on the date shown on the product page; if your order mixes pre-order and in-stock items, everything ships together unless you choose "ship available items first" at checkout.

Customs, duties and import VAT

Shipments to Switzerland and the United Kingdom leave the European Union. For the United Kingdom, UK VAT on orders up to £135 is collected at checkout, so nothing more is due on delivery. For UK orders above £135 and for all Swiss orders, import VAT and any customs duties are paid by the recipient to the carrier on delivery. Some products (for example, raw-meat treats) cannot be shipped to Switzerland or the United Kingdom; they are marked "EU delivery only" on the product page.

Heavy and bulky orders

Orders weighing more than 20 kg in total — for example several large bags of kibble or litter — are delivered by a pallet carrier. A heavy-order surcharge of €5 in France and €10 for other countries is added at checkout, even when the order qualifies for free shipping. The carrier phones you to book a delivery slot, so please make sure your phone number is correct. Pallet deliveries are made to the ground floor or the building entrance.

Tracking your parcel

When your parcel leaves our warehouse you receive an email with a tracking link. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan. You can also find the tracking link under My account → Orders.

If you are not at home, the carrier leaves the parcel at a nearby pick-up point and you have 10 calendar days to collect it. Parcels that are not collected are returned to us; we then refund the order minus the original shipping cost.

Late, lost or damaged parcels

If your parcel has not arrived 5 business days after the latest estimated delivery date, contact our customer care team. We open an investigation with the carrier, which takes up to 10 business days. If the carrier confirms the parcel is lost, we send a free replacement or refund the order in full — your choice.

If a parcel arrives visibly damaged, take photos of the box before opening it and report the damage within 48 hours (see Returns & Refunds, "Damaged or wrong items").

Changing the delivery address

You can change the delivery address yourself until the order status changes to "Preparing". After that, contact us as soon as possible; once a parcel has left the warehouse, the address can only be changed through the carrier's tracking page, where the carrier allows it.
