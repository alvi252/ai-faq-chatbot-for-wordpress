Quick answers to the questions we hear most. Shipping, returns, payments and Autoship have their own pages.

Do you have a physical shop?

No. PawPantry is an online shop only. Every order ships from our logistics centre in Lyon, France.

Do you sell veterinary prescription diets?

No, we do not sell prescription diets. If your vet has prescribed a special diet, please buy it through your vet.

Can I get an invoice with my company's VAT number?

Yes. Enter your company name and VAT number at checkout. The invoice is emailed together with the dispatch confirmation.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes, digital gift cards of €25, €50 and €100. They are sent by email, can be used for any product, and are valid for 2 years from purchase.

Where is PawPantry Kitchen food made?

PawPantry Kitchen dry food is made in France. Accessories come from partner manufacturers in the EU and in Asia; each product page lists the country of origin.

What happens to food close to its best-before date?

Every month we donate unsold food that is close to its best-before date to animal shelters around Lyon.
