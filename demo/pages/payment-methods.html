All prices on our website are shown in euros (EUR) and include VAT for the delivery country. Payment is processed by a certified payment service provider; PawPantry never sees or stores your full card number.

Accepted payment methods

  • Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Carte Bancaire (CB)
  • PayPal
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay (on supported devices and browsers)
  • SEPA bank transfer (see below)
  • PawPantry gift cards
  • Pay in 3 instalments for orders between €90 and €1,500 (cards only, subject to approval by our payment partner)

We do not accept cash on delivery, cheques or cryptocurrencies.

When is my card charged?

When you place an order with a card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, we place an authorisation (a temporary hold) on the order amount. The amount is only charged when your order ships. If part of your order is out of stock, you are charged only for what ships.

If you cancel the order before it ships, the authorisation is released automatically. Depending on your bank, the hold can remain visible on your statement for up to 7 days before it disappears.

Card payments are protected by 3-D Secure: your bank may ask you to confirm the payment in its app or with a one-time code.

Paying by bank transfer

Choose "Bank transfer" at checkout and you will receive our bank details by email. Please use your order number as the payment reference, otherwise we cannot match the payment.

  • We reserve your items for 7 calendar days. If the payment has not arrived by then, the order is cancelled automatically.
  • Your order ships once the money reaches our account, which usually takes 1–2 business days after you send the transfer.
  • Express shipping is not available with bank transfer.

Pay in 3

For orders between €90 and €1,500 you can split the payment into three equal monthly instalments at no extra cost. The first instalment is charged when the order ships, the second and third 30 and 60 days later. Pay in 3 requires a debit or credit card valid for at least 3 more months and is subject to approval by our payment partner. It cannot be combined with Autoship.

Gift cards and store credit

Gift cards are available from €10 to €250 and are valid for 24 months from purchase. You can combine one gift card with any other payment method in the same order. Store credit from refunds appears in your account balance and is applied automatically at checkout, unless you untick it.

Failed or declined payments

If a payment is declined, we do not place the order and nothing is charged. Common causes are an expired card, insufficient funds or a 3-D Secure confirmation that was not completed in time. Please check the details or try another method. For Autoship orders, see Subscription Orders for how failed payments are retried.

Currencies for UK and Swiss customers

Customers in the United Kingdom and Switzerland also pay in euros. Your bank or card provider converts the amount and may charge a currency conversion fee.

Invoices and business customers

A VAT invoice is available under My account → Orders within 24 hours of shipping. To get your company name and VAT number on invoices, add them to your account before ordering; invoices cannot be changed after they are issued.

Verified businesses — breeders, animal shelters, veterinary clinics and pet groomers — can apply for payment by invoice with 30-day terms (net 30) for orders over €300. Write to our business team (see Contact & Opening Hours); approval usually takes 2–3 business days.
