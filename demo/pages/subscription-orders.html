Autoship delivers the food and supplies your pet needs on a schedule you choose, so you never run out. There is no contract, no minimum number of deliveries and no cancellation fee.

Discounts and benefits

  • 25% off your first Autoship order, up to a maximum discount of €20.
  • 10% off every following Autoship order.
  • Free standard shipping in mainland France on Autoship orders from €29. For other destinations, the normal free-shipping thresholds apply (see Shipping & Delivery).

The Autoship discount does not apply to gift cards, and it cannot be combined with other promotion codes. Items that are already on sale get either the sale price or the Autoship discount — whichever is lower for you.

Setting up Autoship

On any eligible product page, choose Autoship instead of "One-time purchase" and pick a frequency: every 1 to 12 weeks. You can mix several products in one Autoship, each with its own frequency; products due within the same week are grouped into one parcel.

Autoship requires a card, PayPal, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Bank transfer and Pay in 3 are not available for Autoship.

Reminder before every delivery

We send a reminder email 3 days before each Autoship order is processed. It lists the products, the price you will pay and the processing date. You do not need to do anything if everything is correct.

Skip, pause, change or cancel

You can manage your Autoship at any time under My account → Autoship:

  • Skip the next delivery with one click; the following one stays on schedule.
  • Pause the whole Autoship for up to 6 months. It restarts automatically at the end of the pause, and we remind you 3 days before.
  • Change products, quantities, frequency or the next processing date.
  • Cancel without any fee.

Changes and cancellations must be made by 23:59 CET on the day before the processing date. Orders that are already being processed cannot be changed, but you can return items under our normal return rules.

Prices

Autoship orders are charged at the product price on the processing day, minus your Autoship discount. If the price of an item in your Autoship has risen by more than 10% since your previous delivery, we tell you in the reminder email so that you can adjust or skip.

Out-of-stock items

If an item is out of stock on the processing day, we ship the rest of your Autoship as planned and skip the missing item for that delivery. You are not charged for it. If the item stays unavailable for two deliveries in a row, we email you a suggestion for a similar product, but we never substitute products without your agreement.

Failed payments

If the payment for an Autoship order fails, we retry automatically after 24 hours and again after 72 hours. If the third attempt also fails, the Autoship is paused and we send you an email so that you can update your payment details. Nothing ships until the payment succeeds.

Moving house or going on holiday

Update your delivery address under My account → Addresses at least one day before the processing date; it applies to all future Autoship orders. Going away? Use Pause rather than cancelling — you keep your Autoship settings, and the 10% discount continues when you resume.
