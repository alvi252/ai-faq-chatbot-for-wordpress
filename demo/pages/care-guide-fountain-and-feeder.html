Regular care keeps your PawPantry Pro devices hygienic, quiet and working for years. Following this guide is also a condition of the warranty (see Product Warranty).

AquaFlow water fountain

The AquaFlow holds 2.5 litres of water and circulates it through a carbon filter with a low-voltage pump.

Filter replacement

  • Replace the carbon filter every 4 weeks.
  • With more than one pet, or if pets shed a lot, replace it every 2 weeks.
  • Rinse a new filter under cold running water for 30 seconds before use.
  • Filters are consumables and are sold in packs of 6.

Weekly cleaning

Once a week, unplug the fountain, empty it and wash the bowl, lid and cover with warm water and a mild, unscented washing-up liquid. Rinse thoroughly — pets dislike the smell of detergent.

The bowl and lid are dishwasher-safe on the top rack, up to 60 °C. The pump, the cable and the power adapter must never go in the dishwasher.

Pump cleaning

Clean the pump every 2 weeks: unplug the fountain, remove the pump cover, take out the impeller (the small magnetic rotor) and rinse it under running water. Use the small brush supplied in the box for the pump housing. A pump that becomes noisy usually just needs this cleaning.

Water level

Keep the water above the MIN line at all times and refill before it drops below it. Running the pump dry overheats it and is not covered by the warranty. With one cat, a full fountain usually lasts 4–6 days.

Hard water

In hard-water areas, descale the fountain once a month: soak the bowl and pump parts for 20 minutes in a 1:1 mix of white vinegar and water, then rinse thoroughly.

SmartBowl automatic feeder

The SmartBowl dispenses dry food on a schedule of up to 6 meals a day, in portions of 5 g to 120 g. The hopper holds 4 litres, about 1.6 kg of kibble. It works with kibble sizes from 3 to 15 mm; wet food, raw food and very oily kibble are not suitable.

Setting up

  1. Insert three D-size batteries as a backup. During a power cut, they keep the feeding schedule running for up to 72 hours.
  2. Connect the power adapter.
  3. Pair the feeder in the PawPantry app. The feeder connects to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only; 5 GHz networks are not supported.
  4. Set the meal times and portion sizes, then run a test portion.

Cleaning

  • Wash the stainless-steel bowl every few days; it is dishwasher-safe.
  • Empty and clean the hopper every 2 weeks with a dry or slightly damp cloth, and let it dry completely before refilling. Moisture makes kibble stick and jam the dispenser.
  • The base contains the motor and electronics: never immerse it in water.

Troubleshooting

  • No food comes out: check the hopper for clumped or oversized kibble and clear the dispenser wheel.
  • Feeder offline in the app: make sure the router uses a 2.4 GHz network and that the feeder is within range.
  • Factory reset: press and hold the setup button for 10 seconds until the light flashes blue. This erases the schedule and Wi-Fi settings.

If the problem persists after these steps, make a warranty claim with a short video showing the issue.
