Our customer care team is based in Lyon, France, and helps with orders, deliveries, returns, product questions and your account. We answer in English, French, German and Spanish.

Customer care hours

Channel Hours (CET)
Live chat Monday–Friday 8:00–20:00, Saturday 9:00–17:00
Phone Monday–Friday 9:00–18:00
Email Answered within 24 hours on business days

We are closed on Sundays and on French public holidays. On 24 and 31 December live chat and phone are open from 9:00 to 13:00 only, and we are closed on 25 December and 1 January.

How to reach us

  • Live chat: click the chat button at the bottom right of any page on pawpantry.example. Chat is the fastest channel; the average waiting time is under 3 minutes.
  • Phone: +33 1 99 00 12 34 (normal national rate from France; international rates may apply from other countries).
  • Email: care@pawpantry.example. Please include your order number so we can help faster.
  • Contact form: My account → Help → Contact us lets you attach photos or videos, which is useful for damaged items and warranty claims.

Outside opening hours, messages sent through chat are turned into email tickets and answered on the next business day.

Click & Collect at our logistics centre

You can collect orders free of charge at our logistics centre:

PawPantry Logistics Centre 18 Rue de l'Exemple 69007 Lyon, France

Collection hours are Monday–Friday 10:00–18:00. Choose "Click & Collect" at checkout; orders placed before 16:00 are usually ready within 2 hours, and we send a text message when your order is ready. Please bring the order confirmation and a photo ID. Orders are kept for 7 days, then cancelled and refunded.

The logistics centre is not a shop: products cannot be bought on site, and returns cannot be dropped off there.

Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how your request was handled, reply to our last email and ask for an escalation. A team lead reviews the case and replies within 3 business days. If we still cannot agree on a solution, you can contact a consumer mediation service free of charge; our customer care team will send you the details on request.
